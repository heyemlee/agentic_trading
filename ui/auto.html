What starting on the real account takes

Improving the strategy

A strategy that has been simulated takes over only after 180 valid sessions, an independent out-of-sample comparison, more after costs than the one in force and than buy-and-hold, and no deeper a drawdown.

Against doing something simpler

The simulated record beside buying and holding the same funds, a simple rule, a 60/40 benchmark and cash, given the same money on the same days.
